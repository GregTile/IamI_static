1. Who We Are & Scope

iamindependent.com, inc. is a United States-based Employer of Record (W-2 umbrella) and back-office management provider serving independent contractors, consultants, and staffing agencies in all 50 U.S. states and internationally. For purposes of applicable data protection laws (including the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and UK GDPR), iamindependent.com, inc. is the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors to our public website (iamindependent.com) and prospective clients who contact us by email, phone, or through our scheduling link. If you onboard as a W-2 employee or enterprise client with IamIndependent, additional employment, payroll, and Master Services Agreement confidentiality terms also apply.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

Our website is an informational site and does not require you to create an account. We collect personal information only when you choose to contact us or book an assessment, such as:

  • Consultation Bookings: Your name, email address, phone number, preferred appointment time, and any notes you choose to provide when scheduling a call through our online scheduling tool.
  • Direct Communications: Contact details and message contents when you email us at info@iamindependent.com or call us at 201-567-2434 (such as your contract rate, start date, or compliance questions).

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you browse our website (subject to your cookie preferences), we automatically collect limited technical and usage information via website analytics tools and standard web server logs, including:

  • Pages viewed, referring website, time spent on pages, and general navigation flow.
  • Device type, operating system, browser type, screen resolution, and approximate geographic region (such as city or state/country).
  • Interactions with site features, such as clicking consultation/phone/email links, expanding FAQ items, or using interactive calculators.

To protect your privacy, our booking confirmation page automatically strips URL query parameters before recording a pageview so personal details appended by external scheduling tools are not transmitted to analytics services.

3. Cookies, Local Storage & Analytics

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website, and browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) allows a site to remember basic settings. We use only the following categories:

Category Purpose Duration
Essential / Functional Remembers your cookie consent preference and prevents duplicate confirmation events within a single browser session. Persistent (preference) / Session
Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors and measures aggregate website traffic, engagement, and performance. Per analytics provider defaults (typically up to 2 years)

Analytics Services & Google Analytics

We use third-party web analytics services, including Google Analytics, to help us understand aggregate traffic and improve our website. These services use cookies and similar technologies to collect usage information. We do not use analytics for cross-site advertising, remarketing, or ad personalization. To learn how Google processes data from partner sites, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Your Choices & How to Opt Out

  • Site Cookie Preferences: You can accept or decline analytics cookies at any time using our cookie preferences control.
  • Browser Controls & Global Privacy Control (GPC): You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Our site also recognizes the browser Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal and defaults analytics storage to denied when GPC is enabled.
Manage Your Preferences Anytime: Want to update or withdraw your analytics cookie preference on this browser?

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule and conduct requested 15-minute assessments and answer your questions about our Employer of Record services.
  • Provide estimates, onboarding documentation, and client/agency compliance information you request.
  • Understand aggregate website usage, evaluate which guides and tools are most helpful, and improve site performance and content.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our website and comply with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.

5. Third-Party Services & Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We also do not share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We work with trusted third-party service providers as needed to operate our website and deliver our services, including:

  • Website Analytics Providers: Tools that help us measure aggregate traffic, site performance, and feature usage.
  • Scheduling & Communication Tools: External platforms used to book consultations, manage calendar invitations, and handle email or phone inquiries (subject to each provider's own privacy notice when you interact with them directly).
  • Hosting, Cloud & IT Infrastructure Providers: Standard hosting, DNS, content delivery, and security providers required to operate our website reliably.
  • Professional Advisors & Legal Disclosures: Attorneys, accountants, or authorities when required by law, court order, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of IamIndependent, our clients, or others.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information you provide through emails, phone calls, or consultations only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, provide requested services, or satisfy legal, accounting, and compliance obligations. Analytics data is retained in accordance with our analytics provider retention settings.

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards—including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and restricted access to client communications—to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Privacy Rights for EEA, UK & Swiss Visitors (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: For placing and reading non-essential analytics cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time via Cookie Preferences without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Pre-Contractual Steps / Contract Performance: When you request an assessment, contract review, or onboarding information.
  • Legitimate Interests: Responding to business inquiries, maintaining website security, and improving our services where not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal Obligation: Complying with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to and a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Request restriction of processing or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request data portability in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

International Data Transfers: Because IamIndependent is based in the United States, information you provide or that is processed by our third-party providers may be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required, our service providers rely on recognized transfer mechanisms such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. U.S. State Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA & Other States)

Residents of California (under the CCPA/CPRA) and other U.S. states with comprehensive privacy laws (including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, New Jersey, and others) may have certain rights regarding their personal information, including the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information we collect, use, and disclose, and request access to specific pieces of personal information.
  • Request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your privacy rights.

As stated above, iamindependent.com, inc. does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To submit a privacy request, please contact us using the details in Section 10 below. We will verify your request before fulfilling it as required by law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, or applicable laws. When we make updates, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
