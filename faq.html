Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about transitioning to contracting, managing taxes, and using an umbrella Employer of Record service.

iamindependent, inc. is a business processing outsourcer and umbrella Employer of Record (EOR). We become your legal employer of record. Essentially, you work as an independent contractor, choosing your projects and clients, but you receive all of the benefits associated with a traditional W-2 employee (statutory protections, 401k, direct deposit) plus all the tax advantages of an independent contractor — including immediate reimbursement for IRS-approved business expenses instead of waiting until you file annual taxes.

No, you do not need to form an LLC, S-Corp, or any legal entity. Because IAI acts as your W-2 Employer of Record, you contract directly through our umbrella. This saves you thousands of dollars in formation fees, registered agent costs, state franchise taxes, separate business bank account fees, and corporate tax preparation costs.

Under our IRS-compliant Accountable Plan, legitimate expenses you incur for your contracting work (such as developer hardware, monitors, home office internet, mobile phone, software licenses, and cloud labs) are submitted through our portal. These expenses are reimbursed to you pre-tax out of your gross client billing before payroll taxes are withheld. You save money immediately on each paycheck.

You are fully covered under IAI's master commercial policies: Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance, Commercial General Liability Insurance, and Workers' Compensation Insurance across all 50 states. We provide Certificates of Insurance (COI) to your clients and staffing vendors within hours of onboarding.

Yes. As a W-2 employee under our umbrella, you gain access to our corporate group 401(k) retirement plan. You can make pre-tax salary deferrals directly from your paychecks, allowing you to continue growing your retirement nest egg without having to set up and administer a Solo 401(k) yourself.

Yes, enterprise clients and staffing agencies routinely prefer working with an established Employer of Record like IamIndependent. It eliminates their risk of co-employment and completely shields them from IRS 1099 independent contractor reclassification audits. We handle the Master Service Agreement (MSA) and standard vendor onboarding seamlessly.

We manage all billing and accounts receivable. Our service managers follow up professionally with your client’s accounts payable department so you never have to act as a debt collector or damage your working relationship with your project team.
