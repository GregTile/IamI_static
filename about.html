Pioneers in Back-Office Management for Independent Professionals

iamindependent was founded in 2000 in response to the growing market need to cut contingent labor costs, eliminate co-employment risk, and remove the liability of independent contractor reclassification.

Our Mission & Origin

Simultaneously as corporate contingent labor grew, the Independent Contractor Community was burgeoning. More self-employed professionals were taking full responsibility for their talent, earning capacity, and how they worked.

iamindependent was formed by Steven Wasserstrom, CFA, CPA and Nathan Fink, Esq., CPA, in order to ease the working relationships between independent contractors and the organizations they provide services to.

Today, Mr. Wasserstrom and Mr. Fink lead a team of dedicated service managers trained in understanding and providing independents with the most up-to-date legal, accounting, benefits, and administrative tools to help them succeed. The benefits to entities who hire contract talent has helped establish IAI as a premier partner in 1099 compliance and IRS conformity across all 50 states and internationally.

Executive Leadership

Steven Wasserstrom, CFA, CPA

Founder & CEO

Prior to co-founding iamindependent, Mr. Wasserstrom was President of Contractor’s Resources, which pioneered the concept of organizing and providing back-office administrative operations and group benefits to independent professionals throughout the United States — an entity structure that became known as an "umbrella company".

Mr. Wasserstrom has nearly four decades of professional financial experience as a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) and a Certified Public Accountant (CPA). He taught and prepared many of Wall Street’s top analysts for their CFA examinations. He is a senior member of the American Financial Association, the New York Society of Security Analysts, and the New Jersey Society of CPAs.

Before Contractor’s Resources, Mr. Wasserstrom worked on Wall Street as a research analyst, portfolio manager, arbitrageur, and owner of his own financial firm, which was a member of the Philadelphia Stock Exchange.

Nathan Fink, Esq., CPA

Co-Founder & CEO

Mr. Fink is a co-founder of iamindependent and provides leadership to the company in the critical areas of Law, Compliance, and Accounting.

He is a member of the New Jersey Bar and the New Jersey Society of CPAs. He performed his Law Review on Domestic and International Sales Corporations. He earned his CPA from the State of New York, his Juris Doctorate (JD) from Loyola University, and completed his Master of Laws (LLM) at New York University School of Law.

Mr. Fink has operated his own legal and tax accounting practice since 1978. With experienced, keen legal, accounting, and financial minds at the helm, IAI is the preferred business management service for contractors, consultants, and the corporations that engage contingent workforces.
