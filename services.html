Executive-Level Umbrella Services for Independent Professionals

You may be new to contracting or consulting, or a seasoned pro who already reaps the benefits of controlling your career and earnings. As an independent professional, the financial rewards can be great — as long as you have the right business management service to administer your back office and offer you an umbrella of services.

At iamindependent (IAI), we combine decades of Wall Street finance, CPA accounting, and tax law expertise to help you succeed. IAI becomes your employer of record, giving you executive benefits, statutory protections, and maximum take-home pay.

Core Foundation

Employer of Record (W-2 Umbrella)

When clients or staffing agencies hire you, they require an entity that carries full statutory employment obligations. Instead of forcing you through a restrictive agency markup or making you form an expensive solo corporation, IAI acts as your compliant Employer of Record.

  • ✓ Work for yourself without solo corporate tax returns or state franchise fees
  • ✓ Standard W-2 tax reporting with zero self-employment tax surprises
  • ✓ Full compliance in all 50 US states and international contracting engagements

Why Tech Workers Love This

You get the prestigious title of an independent consultant, set your own hourly or project rates, and select which clients you take. IAI handles all back-office tax withholding, FICA, federal/state compliance, and quarterly reporting.

  • • Developer laptops, monitors, standing desks & ergonomic gear

  • • Continuing technical education, books, and certifications
  • • Business travel, lodging, and client-related meals
Financial Advantage

Immediate Pre-Tax Expense Reimbursement

Traditional W-2 employees can no longer deduct unreimbursed employee expenses on federal returns. Solo 1099 contractors must wait until filing their tax returns in April to offset expenses.

Under IAI’s IRS-compliant Accountable Expense Plan, your legitimate business expenses are reimbursed to you tax-free on each pay cycle directly from your gross billings. This immediately lowers your taxable gross income, putting hundreds or thousands of dollars back into your pocket every single month.

Enterprise Protection

Professional Insurances (E&O, Liability & Workers' Comp)

Most enterprise clients (banks, healthcare, tech giants) and staffing vendors mandate that contractors carry at least $1,000,000 to $5,000,000 in Errors & Omissions (E&O) and Commercial General Liability coverage.

As an individual, shopping for specialty tech E&O insurance is frustrating and can cost between $2,000 and $4,000 annually. With IAI, you are automatically covered under our comprehensive master policies:

  • ✓ Comprehensive Errors & Omissions (E&O) Insurance
  • ✓ Commercial General Liability Insurance
  • ✓ Statutory Workers' Compensation Insurance across all jurisdictions

Instant Certificate of Insurance (COI)

When your client’s vendor management team asks for proof of insurance, we issue your Certificate of Insurance naming your client or agency as an additional insured within hours. No delays, no unexpected premiums.

Never Chase an Unpaid Invoice Again

Solo freelancers spend an average of 15% of their working hours dealing with accounting admin and following up on aging accounts receivable. IAI gives you that time back to focus entirely on billable development.

Back-Office Automation

Invoicing, Collections & Direct Deposit

Managing vendor payment terms (Net-30, Net-60), sending purchase order invoices, and chasing accounts payable departments is draining.

IAI takes over your complete billing pipeline:

  • ✓ Timesheet collection and client invoice generation
  • ✓ Accounts receivable tracking and polite, persistent collections follow-up
  • ✓ Seamless Direct Deposit of net payroll to your personal bank account
Wealth Building

Group 401(k) Retirement Plan & Statutory Benefits

Leaving full-time employment should never mean pausing your retirement savings. IamIndependent offers access to an executive-level 401(k) retirement program that allows you to contribute substantial pre-tax earnings toward your future.

You also retain statutory protections including state disability and unemployment compensation insurance, providing an important financial safety net between engagements.

Long-Term Financial Peace of Mind

Our founders — a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA/CPA) and an attorney (Esq./CPA) — structured our umbrella service so you build long-term wealth without getting penalized for being independent.
